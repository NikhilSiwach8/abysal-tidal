What these are
--------------
The thumbnails players see in the casino lobby. They must read at a glance at
small size: one clear scene, strong contrast, the logo large, nothing busy.

   T1  4:3   (e.g. 1600x1200)   the harpooner and the Leviathan face to face
   T2  16:9  (e.g. 1920x1080)   the same moment, wider, more sea and sky

Each comes in two passes:
   A  ART ONLY (no text): generate this. The logo is added afterwards from
      our own logo file, so the lettering is always spelled and styled right.
   B  WITH LOGO (optional): only if you want the tool to place the logo itself.

Do not generate the words "ABYSSAL TIDE" with an image tool for the final tile:
image tools misspell. Send me the art-only image and I will place our real logo
(art/generated/lettering/logo_abyssal_tide.png) on it at the correct size.

 HOW TO KEEP IT CONSISTENT (read once)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Attach your STYLE ANCHOR (prompt 0.1) as the style reference.
     Midjourney: --sref <anchor-url> --sw 200
     GPT-Image / Ideogram / Leonardo / Flux: attach it as a style reference.
2. Attach the hero reference sheet and the Leviathan reference sheet as
   character references (Midjourney: --cref <url> --cw 100), so both look
   exactly like they do in the game.
3. Keep one seed for both tiles, so T1 and T2 look like one campaign.
4. Leave the LOGO ZONE empty and calm (described in each prompt). Busy detail
   behind the logo makes it unreadable at thumbnail size.
5. Colour rule (same as the game): 90% greyscale ink and fog, with only teal
   #2BB3A3 / #5FE3D2 accents (hero's eyes and scarf, Leviathan's gills, the
   water) and a little ember coral #E8553E (blood, embers). No other colours.

Palette (exact)
   Ink black #171B1D   Ink grey #222A2D   Fog paper #DCDFD7
   Abyss teal #2BB3A3  Teal glow #5FE3D2  Ember coral #E8553E   Gold #FFD36B
