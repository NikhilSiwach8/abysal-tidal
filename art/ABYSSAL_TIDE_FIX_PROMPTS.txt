 READ FIRST: why the checkerboards happened and how to avoid them
---------------------------------------------------------------------
If a tool cannot export real transparency, the words "transparent background"
make it PAINT a grey-and-white checkerboard into the picture. It looks
transparent in a preview but is solid pixels, so it shows up in the game.

Use whichever applies to your tool:

  1. Tool HAS a real transparency option (GPT-Image "background: transparent",
     Ideogram / Leonardo "transparent background" toggle, Photoshop Firefly
     "remove background"): turn the OPTION on, and keep the prompt sentence
     "isolated on a transparent background". Export PNG (not JPG / WebP-lossy).

  2. Tool has NO transparency option (Midjourney, most Flux / SDXL front-ends):
     do NOT write "transparent". Use the chroma-key sentence instead:
       "isolated on a perfectly flat solid pure green #00FF00 background with no
        shadows, no gradient, no texture, no floor"
     Then remove the green with any chroma-key / background-removal tool.
     Pure green is used because the art contains white teeth, foam, bubbles and
     pale belly plates; a white or checkered background would be eaten together
     with them. No asset in this game uses pure green.

  3. Always check the result: open it in an editor, the corners must be
     alpha 0. If you see a checkerboard pattern INSIDE the image (e.g. in holes
     of torn fins or cloth), it is painted — reject it.

Every prompt below has two interchangeable background lines:
  [ALPHA]  for tools with a transparency option
  [GREEN]  for tools without one
Paste the prompt and keep only ONE of the two lines.

Reference images: attach your own approved images as references (they are
original Abyssal Tide art):
  STYLE   art/generated/background/bg_composite.webp        (style anchor)
  HERO    art/generated/hero/hero_reference_sheet.webp      (character ref)
  IDLE    art/generated/hero/hero_idle.webp                 (pose/canvas ref)
  LEVI    art/generated/leviathan/leviathan_reference_sheet.webp
  LIDLE   art/generated/leviathan/leviathan_idle.webp
Midjourney: --sref <STYLE url> --sw 200, and --cref <HERO or LEVI url> --cw 100.
Other tools: attach as "style reference" / "character reference".
