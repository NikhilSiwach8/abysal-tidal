=====================================================================
 ABYSSAL TIDE — GAME DESCRIPTION
=====================================================================

SHORT (one line)
----------------
Hunt the Leviathan through the deep: a 6×6 cluster-pays tumble slot where every
win wounds the beast, unleashing power-ups, multipliers up to 64× and wins up to
100,000×.


FULL DESCRIPTION
----------------
Beneath the waves, a hooded harpooner stalks the Leviathan.

Abyssal Tide is a 6×6 cluster-pays slot. Land 5 or more matching symbols
touching horizontally or vertically to win. Winning symbols burst and new ones
tumble in, chaining win after win.

Wound the Leviathan. Every winning symbol deals damage. Every 25 damage
unleashes the next of eight power-ups, from Tidal Turn and Whirlpool Block to
Twin Currents. At 200 damage the Leviathan rises as a giant mega symbol up to
6×6.

Whirlpool multipliers. Every cell hides a multiplier spot. A winning cluster
with a Whirlpool Wild doubles the spots beneath it, up to 64×.

Free spins in four depths. Land 3 or more Sunken Compass scatters for 10 free
spins. The more compasses, the deeper you go: Riptide, Undertow, Maelstrom, and
Abyssal, where every spot starts at 8×. Spots and damage carry across the whole
feature.

Choose your hunt. Raise your odds with the Leviathan Hunt or Trench Hunt, buy
straight into a free-spin depth, or spin the Depth Wheel with a Mystery buy.

RTP 96.70% · Max win 100,000×


FEATURE LIST
------------
- 6×6 grid, cluster pays (5+), tumbling reels
- Leviathan damage meter: a power-up every 25 damage, 8 in total
- The Leviathan rises at 200 damage as a mega symbol up to 6×6
- Multiplier spots under every cell, doubled by Wild clusters, up to 64×
- Four free-spin depths with starting multipliers of 1×, 2×, 4× and 8×
- Bonus buys, Hunt modes and the Mystery Depth Wheel
- RTP 96.70% in every mode, max win 100,000×
