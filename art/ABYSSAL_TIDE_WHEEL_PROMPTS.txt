How the wheel is built (read once)
----------------------------------
The slice SIZES are the real odds and differ between the two buys:
   Mystery Depths   Still Water 60%  Undertow 25%  Maelstrom 10%  Abyssal 5%
   Abyssal Mystery  Still Water 60%  Undertow 30%                 Abyssal 10%
Image generators cannot draw exact angles, so do NOT generate a finished
wheel with slices. The game draws the slices in code at the exact sizes and
lays your painted parts over and under them:

   W1 rim ring (frame + pegs)      on top of the slices
   W2 slice wash textures (x4)     clipped into each slice in code
   W3 tier emblems (x4)            one small emblem inside each slice
   W4 centre hub cap               centre of the wheel
   W5 pointer                      12 o'clock, does not spin
   W6 back glow / burst            behind the wheel
   W7 "THE DEPTH WHEEL" lettering  title above the wheel
   W8 "STILL WATER" lettering      result title for no bonus
   W9 landing flash sprite sheet   plays on the winning slice
   W10 idle + spin animation       optional video loops

Consistency rules (same as the main prompt file)
   * Use your STYLE ANCHOR (prompt 0.1) as the style reference for every image:
       Midjourney  --sref <anchor-url> --sw 200
       GPT-Image / Ideogram / Leonardo / Flux: attach the anchor as style reference.
   * Use the existing buy-menu "mystery" illustration (question mark + sealed
     chest) as a second reference for W4, so the hub matches the buy card.
   * Use the existing title lettering (UNDERTOW / MAELSTROM / ABYSSAL) as the
     reference for W7 and W8, so all titles share one brush hand.
   * Keep one seed for the whole wheel family.
   * Light always soft from the top left. Same palette hex codes, no new colours.

Palette (exact values)
   Ink black        #171B1D     Ink grey        #222A2D
   Fog paper        #DCDFD7     Fog paper dark  #C9CEC6
   Abyss teal       #2BB3A3     Teal glow       #5FE3D2
   Ember coral      #E8553E     Gold            #FFD36B
   Abyssal purple   #A26FF0     Highlight white #F3F5F2
Tier colours on the wheel (match the title lettering slashes):
   Still Water = ink grey #222A2D     Undertow = gold #FFD36B
   Maelstrom   = abyss teal #2BB3A3   Abyssal  = abyssal purple #A26FF0

Transparency (avoid painted checkerboards)
   Turn ON the tool's real transparent-background option and export PNG.
   If your tool has none, replace "isolated on a transparent background" with
   "isolated on a flat pure white #FFFFFF background" (W1, W3, W4, W5, W7-W9)
   or "on a flat pure black #000000 background" (W6 glow), and I key it out.

NEGATIVE (paste into the negative field, or append "Avoid: ..." for tools
without one):
text, letters, numbers, watermark, signature, logo, photorealistic, 3D render,
plastic, glossy CGI, chrome, metallic bevel, casino roulette, playing cards,
dice, poker chips, neon, rainbow colours, oversaturated, lens flare, blurry,
low resolution, jpeg artifacts, checkerboard pattern, samurai, ninja, oni mask,
katana, bamboo, torii gate, pagoda, cherry blossoms, Japanese mask.
(The last line keeps us away from the reference game's subject matter.)
