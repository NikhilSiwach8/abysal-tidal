What changes
------------
The five low symbols become card ranks, painted in the same ink-wash style as
the rest of the game. Pay order and colour coding stay as they are, so the math
does not change and players keep their colour cues:

   Slot  Old symbol   New   Colour wash (unchanged)       Small sea ornament
   L1    seahorse     10    sea-green  #5FBFA8           kelp ribbon
   L2    starfish     J     ember coral #E8553E          knotted rope
   L3    coral        Q     rose coral #E07A86           pearl
   L4    shell        K     sand ochre #E7A94B           anchor fluke
   L5    pearl        A     pale sea blue #8FB8EA        fish-hook barb

L1 pays least, L5 most (A is the top low symbol). Ornaments stay small and in
black ink, so the letters read first and nothing looks like a high symbol,
the Wild, or the compass scatter.

   use "on a flat pure white #FFFFFF background" instead and I will key it out.

   and keep a clean one, or use the FALLBACK at the end of this file.

Palette (exact): ink black #171B1D, highlight white #F3F5F2, plus the one wash
colour listed for that symbol. Soft light from the top left.
